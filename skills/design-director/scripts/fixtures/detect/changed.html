Day one. Fog until noon, then a clear run to the second cairn. Eleven readings.

Day two. Wind from the west all day. The upper line had to wait; four readings.

Day three. Clear and cold. Finished the upper line and walked out by dusk.

Notes from the north ridge

Three days of walking the survey line, written up each evening by lamp. The readings are rough; the weather was worse.

All readings are kept in the bound ledger. These notes only summarise them.

Survey season, autumn.
