# Marketplace icon
Chosen: **B, two rows** — a slab `D` over a smaller `E S` above one acuity rule
(`variants/r2-wide-t38.svg`). The only candidate that reads as an eye chart at 48 px and up and
still holds at 16 px, where the `D` is solid and `E S` stays two distinct marks.
Font: **Rockwell Bold** (`/System/Library/Fonts/Supplemental/Rockwell.ttc`) — the face the social
preview sets its optotypes in, so the match is exact. Glyphs are outlined; no font is loaded.
Final: `design-director-bca059af.svg` (sha256 of `icon.svg`, identical content), 1.7 KB, 256×256
viewBox, one `fill="currentColor"`, three paths and one rect, no script or remote reference.
Ink spans 89% across and 91% down, lifted 4 units above the geometric centre because the rule
bottom-weights the stack. The mark is height-bound, so its width comes from the rule and the row
tracking — the preview's own chart proportion, and what stops `E S` merging at 16 px. The rule is
16 units tall on a multiple of 16, landing on a whole pixel at 16 px, not two half-lit ones.
Rejected: **A** (one huge `D` + rule), crisp but a letter with an underline, not a chart; **C**
(`D`/`E S`/`I G N`), best at 128 px, a smear at 16 and 24. `icon-2color.png` skipped — baked warm
white cannot adapt to a dark listing. Every candidate's size ladder is in `variants/`.
